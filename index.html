About the Evening

The First Annual September Standard

An intimate black-tie gala at 74Wythe in Williamsburg, Brooklyn, bringing together 400 friends, families, and supporters for an evening of live music, dancing, and a live raffle. Doors open at 8:00 PM and close at 9:45 PM — please plan to arrive early. There will be no food served at the event, so please eat before you arrive.

The open bar runs from 9:00 PM to 12:00 AM, with a cash bar available before and after. The night wraps at 12:15 AM, followed by an after-party at a location to be announced.

All proceeds benefit the Pediatric Center at Memorial Sloan Kettering, one of the world's foremost cancer research and treatment institutions. This is our community's way of holding the line for the families and researchers who need it most.
